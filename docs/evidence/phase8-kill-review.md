## Rows 12 and 13: owner decision

Decision (2026-11-12, owner via the conductor): **keep both, remove
nothing.** The owner ordered both built against fakes, they stay inert
without approval, and 8.4 removes only owner-approved features. Revisit when
a second real route or a real tracker exists, or when the skipped proofs run.

The options weighed:

| Option                                 | Code                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | UI change needed                                                                                               |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Keep both (recommended)                | None. The seam is inert: nothing loads without a `plugins` entry and a person's approval of the exact version and content hash                                                                                                                                                                                | None                                                                                                           |
| Cut both back to schema                | Remove loading and dispatch from `src/app/plugins.mjs`, `plugin-child.mjs`, `plugin-session.mjs`, `trackers.mjs`, and their calls in `routes.mjs`, `tick.mjs`, `operations.mjs`, `page-views.mjs`, `layered-config.mjs`; drop `proof-p26.test.mjs` and the fixtures; keep `plugin-contract.mjs` as the schema | Yes: `kirocrew-app/ui/src/PluginsSettings.tsx`, `plugins-api.ts`, `test/plugins-api.test.mjs` (from `773b2f2`) |
| Cut only the tracker contract (row 13) | Remove `trackers.mjs` and its calls; keep routes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Possibly: the tracker parts of `PluginsSettings.tsx`                                                           |

Why keep: the owner ordered this build knowing the gate was not met
(`p26-fake-plugins.md`). The criterion protects against carrying an unused
seam; here the seam costs no runtime risk while off, P26 passes, and
removing it throws away work the owner asked for. The criterion stays live:
if no real route exists by release, cutting back is still open.
